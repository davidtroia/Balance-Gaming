Mardi Gras Mayhem

It's Fat Tuesday in the French Quarter, and King Gator rules the parade. Catch gold doubloons to fill the Hold & Win board, land three Free Spins symbols to join the parade, and every Wild that marches in raises your multiplier.

Sound on. Play credits only.

How to play

1,024 ways. Match 3 or more symbols on adjacent reels from the left. Wilds land on reels 2 to 4 and stand in for every pay symbol.

Krewe Wilds. At random, whole reels turn Wild in the base game.

Free Spins parade. 3, 4 or 5 Free Spins symbols award 8, 12 or 20 free spins. Every Wild that lands raises the parade multiplier by 1x, up to 10x, for the rest of the feature. 3 Free Spins symbols during the feature add 5 more spins.

Doubloon Hold & Win. 6 or more gold doubloons lock in place for 3 respins. Each new doubloon resets the respins to 3. Mini, Minor and Major doubloons pay their jackpot. Fill all 20 spaces for the Grand.

Return to player 96.25%, measured over 50 million simulated rounds. Max win 5,000x bet. Every spin is random and independent; past results never change the next spin.

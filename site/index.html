Features

Krewe Wilds

About 1 in 130 base rounds, one, two or all three middle reels turn fully wild. It keeps the base game lively between features.

Free Spins Parade

Three, four or five scatters award 8, 12 or 20 free spins. Every Wild that lands adds +1× to a parade multiplier that holds for the whole feature, up to 10×. Retriggers add five spins.

Doubloon Hold & Win

Six or more gold doubloons lock in place for three respins, and each new coin resets the count. Coins carry cash values or one of four fixed jackpots. Fill all 20 cells and the Grand pays 1,000×.

Sound built in-house

A custom New Orleans soundtrack with brass, parade drums and a game-show host who calls out the big moments. Celebrations scale with the size of the win.

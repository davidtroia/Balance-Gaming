How to play

1,024 ways. Match 3+ symbols on adjacent reels from the left. Wilds (reels 2–4) substitute for all pay symbols.

Climbing Wilds. At random, whole reels climb to Wild in the base game.

Summit Free Spins. 3, 4 or 5 Summits award 8, 12 or 20 free spins. Every Wild that lands raises the Altitude multiplier by 1x, up to 10x, for the rest of the feature.

Treasure Hold & Win. 6+ Gold Coins lock in place for 3 respins. Each new coin resets the respins to 3. Fill all 20 spaces for the Grand.

Return to player 96.2% (simulated over 50 million rounds). Max win 5,000x bet. Results are random and independent; nothing that happened before changes the next spin.

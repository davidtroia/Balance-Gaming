GOLDENASCENT

Legend says a golden eagle guards the treasure of the highest peak. Climb with it through the storm. Every Summit you find lifts you higher, every Wild raises your altitude, and Gold Coins open the treasure vault.

Sound on. Play credits only.

How to play

1,024 ways. Match 3 or more symbols on adjacent reels from the left. Wilds land on reels 2 to 4 and stand in for every pay symbol.

Climbing Wilds. At random, whole reels climb to Wild in the base game.

Summit Free Spins. 3, 4 or 5 Summits award 8, 12 or 20 free spins. Every Wild that lands raises your altitude multiplier by 1x, up to 10x, for the rest of the feature. 3 Summits during the feature add 5 more spins.

Treasure Hold & Win. 6 or more Gold Coins lock in place for 3 respins. Each new coin resets the respins to 3. Mini, Minor and Major coins pay their jackpot. Fill all 20 spaces for the Grand.

Return to player 96.25%, measured over 50 million simulated rounds. Max win 5,000x bet. Every spin is random and independent; past results never change the next spin.

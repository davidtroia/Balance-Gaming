How to play

Mardi Gras Mayhem · Player guide

Welcome to the parade

Match 3 or more of the same symbol on adjacent reels, starting from the leftmost reel. There are 1,024 ways to win: a symbol counts anywhere on its reel, so you don't need a line. Each win pays its table amount once for every way it lands, and all wins on a spin are added together.

Game controls

SpinStarts the reels. You can also press the space bar.
Bet − / +Lowers or raises your total bet per spin.
  1. Set your bet. Use BET − and BET + beside the bet meter. Every prize in this guide scales with your bet.
  2. Spin the parade. Tap SPIN. The reels stop left to right.
  3. Collect your wins. Winning symbols light up and the WIN meter counts up. Big wins get the crowd going.
  4. Chase the features. Free Spins symbols, gold doubloons and Krewe Wilds open the bonus rounds in the other tabs.

Wilds land on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stand in for every pay symbol. They don't replace Free Spins symbols or doubloons.

Krewe Wilds

On any base game spin, the krewe can march in at random and turn whole reels Wild before the spin is paid. Wild reels count as the symbol you need on every row.

Free Spins parade

  1. Trigger. Land 3, 4 or 5 Free Spins symbols anywhere for 8, 12 or 20 free spins. Free Spins symbols also pay on their own.
  2. Parade multiplier. Every Wild that lands raises the multiplier by 1x, up to 10x. It never drops for the rest of the feature.
  3. Retrigger. 3 Free Spins symbols during the feature add 5 more spins.

Free spins play at the bet that triggered them.

Doubloon Hold & Win

  1. Trigger. Land 6 or more gold doubloons on one spin.
  2. Respins. The doubloons lock in place and you get 3 respins. Every new doubloon locks too and resets the respins to 3.
  3. Jackpots. Mini, Minor and Major doubloons pay their jackpot. Fill all 20 spaces to win the Grand.
  4. Payout. When the respins run out, every locked doubloon pays its value.
